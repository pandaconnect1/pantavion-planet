# Pantavion Deep Research and Unification Policy

Status: MANDATORY CANONICAL POLICY  
Applies to: every Pantavion module, submodule, capability, migration and innovation claim.

## Mandatory rule

No module may move directly from recovered material or an idea into implementation. Every work unit must first pass a documented deep-research and unification review.

## Required research dossier per module

1. **Recovered internal truth**
   - all related corpus records, branches, donor artifacts and prior specifications;
   - exact provenance, duplicate relations and conflicting versions;
   - existing code, APIs, schemas, UI, tests, deployments and live evidence;
   - gaps, blockers, unfinished work and unclassified material.

2. **Capability decomposition**
   - user problems and intended outcomes;
   - capabilities and sub-capabilities;
   - actors, roles, permissions and consent;
   - workflows, states, failure modes and recovery paths;
   - dependencies on the Pantavion shared core.

3. **External international research**
   - comparable global products and public implementations;
   - authoritative technical standards and protocols;
   - relevant peer-reviewed or institutional research;
   - country/region laws and regulatory obligations;
   - accessibility, safety, minors, privacy and ethical requirements;
   - language, dialect, cultural and low-connectivity variants;
   - documented strengths, weaknesses and unmet needs.

4. **Seven-continent matrix**
   - Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, South America, Oceania and remote/Antarctic operations;
   - country/territory and subdivision differences where material;
   - language, script, locale, culture, accessibility and network readiness;
   - regional authorities, emergency routes, data residency and retention.

5. **Unification design**
   - reuse shared identity, consent, safety, translation, graph, storage, audit and runtime services;
   - retain unique recovered material;
   - merge semantic duplicates through canonical references, never silent deletion;
   - document conflicts and the selected canonical disposition;
   - avoid duplicated data, code and parallel competing implementations.

6. **Innovation and funding evidence**
   - problem, novelty hypothesis and Pantavion differentiator;
   - comparison against existing approaches;
   - evidence pointers and implementation boundary;
   - measurable technical, social and beneficiary outcomes;
   - maturity, risks, mitigations, IP and freedom-to-operate review;
   - claims remain unverified until substantiated.

7. **Implementation and verification**
   - canonical schemas, APIs, UI and operational controls;
   - unit, integration, security, accessibility and end-to-end tests;
   - production deployment evidence and regional test matrix;
   - monitoring, rollback, incident handling and audit evidence.

## Required truth states

PRESERVED → DEEPLY_ANALYZED → CLASSIFIED → RESEARCHED → UNIFICATION_DESIGNED → CANONICALIZED → IMPLEMENTED → TESTED → DEPLOYED → VERIFIED_LIVE.

A later state may never be inferred from the existence of files, titles, screenshots, workflows or specifications.

## Evidence requirements

Every dossier must contain:

- date and scope;
- corpus fingerprint and record IDs;
- primary/authoritative external sources with links;
- assumptions and unresolved questions;
- decisions and rejected alternatives;
- exact implementation references;
- test results and production evidence;
- owner-visible status and next action.

## Safety and deletion

- Original material remains preserved throughout research and implementation.
- Exact duplicates are linked, not deleted automatically.
- No source repository, branch, file or record may be deleted until the complete migration is reconciled, tested, verified and explicitly approved by the owner.
- Security, privacy and legal constraints override speed.

## Completion definition

A module is complete only when its required capability set is implemented, integrated with the shared core, tested across relevant regional variants, deployed, observed in production and supported by dated evidence.

Labels such as COMPLETE, GLOBAL, SECURE, INNOVATIVE or VERIFIED_LIVE are prohibited without matching evidence.
