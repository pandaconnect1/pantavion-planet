## Purpose
Pressure-test the remaining Human Need Truth Separation & Continuity Invariant (HNTSCI) against goal-oriented requirements engineering, case management, process compliance, audit/history, outcome tracking, and interoperability patterns.

## Candidate under attack
The candidate requires explicit non-equivalence and non-overwrite among:

1. original human expression
2. AI/system inference or interpretation
3. independently verified context
4. rights / consent / jurisdiction / authority state
5. proposed outcome or action
6. authorization
7. actual execution/effect
8. evidence of execution/effect
9. human-reported satisfaction
10. residual/unresolved human need

The hypothesis additionally requires versioned provenance and continuity when execution moves across model, agent, application, provider, device, language, or jurisdiction boundaries.

## Prior-art pressure found

### Goal-oriented requirements engineering
GODA and contextual/runtime goal-model work already model context-dependent goal fulfillment and check fulfillment constraints against system execution traces. Therefore context-aware goal satisfaction, runtime goal checking, and execution-trace comparison are not independently novel.

### Goal/process compliance
2026 goal-model-based compliance work maps process activities to goal elements and evaluates weak, strong, and monotonic satisfaction with traceability. Therefore process-to-goal traceability and evidence-based compliance are not independently novel.

### Case management
Government case-management standards already require goals/outcomes, dynamic routing, information controls, auditing and workflow oversight. Microsoft government/industry case-management reference models include Case, Person/Party, Task, Event/Interaction, Decision, Relationship, Outcome and Audit/History, together with interoperability and client-satisfaction measures. Therefore a persistent case containing person, actions, decisions, outcomes and audit history is not independently novel.

### Auditable outcome records
Existing case-management systems preserve decisions, evidence, chronology, status and audit trails. Therefore immutable/history-aware outcome evidence and recoverable case timelines are not independently novel.

## What is eliminated as a standalone novelty claim
- goal lifecycle
- runtime goal satisfaction
- context-dependent goal fulfillment
- goal-to-execution traceability
- case lifecycle
- person + task + decision + outcome + audit/history record
- dynamic case routing
- evidence-backed decisions
- client/user satisfaction measurement
- interoperability across case-management systems
- immutable/versioned audit trails by themselves

## Remaining exact-combination hypothesis
The search in this attack did not identify a single reference that clearly requires all ten truth states to remain semantically non-equivalent and non-overwriting throughout a human-need lifecycle, while also preserving the unresolved residual need as the continuity anchor across heterogeneous AI/model/agent/app/provider/device/language/jurisdiction transitions.

That absence is NOT novelty proof. It only means this exact combination survives this search pass.

## Critical distinction
A conventional case can record goals, decisions, evidence and outcomes. HNTSCI is only potentially differentiable if the implementation enforces invariants such as:

- inferred_intent MUST NOT overwrite human_expression
- authorization MUST NOT prove human desire
- execution MUST NOT imply successful outcome
- system-measured success MUST NOT overwrite human-reported satisfaction
- case/workflow closure MUST NOT erase residual_need
- provider/model handoff MUST preserve semantic identity and provenance of every state
- later corrections MUST append/version rather than silently rewrite the historical truth chain
