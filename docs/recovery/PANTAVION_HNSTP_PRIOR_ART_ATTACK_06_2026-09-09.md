# Pantavion HNSTP — Prior-Art Attack 06

Status: RESEARCH_ONLY / NOVELTY_UNVERIFIED
Date: 2026-09-09

## Question attacked
Can Pantavion claim novelty merely because it models a persistent human need/state and adapts technology around it?

## Findings
No. The search found substantial adjacent prior art:

1. Needs-aware AI literature explicitly calls for AI systems that identify, prioritize, measure and satisfy human needs while remaining human-aware, accountable, lawful and ethical.
2. Human Digital Twin research represents human needs, abilities, acceptance and contextual/physiological/cognitive state so technology can adapt dynamically.
3. Intelligent-environment research already orchestrates multiple technologies around human activities/needs and adapts runtime support from context.
4. Living-software research proposes translating human goals into computational actions and dynamically evolving software around changing user needs/context.
5. Patent literature describes real-time context-aware assistants that anticipate evolving needs/preferences, adapt accessibility/UI, integrate third-party services, and act across multiple life domains.
6. Personalized-agent patent literature includes translating user intention into forms suitable for previously unknown external software services.

Therefore the following are NOT sufficient novelty claims by themselves:
- needs-aware AI;
- persistent user/human state;
- adaptive human digital twin;
- context-aware service orchestration;
- software adapting to goals/needs;
- proactive personal assistant;
- cross-service/API execution;
- accessibility-aware personalization.

## Surviving Pantavion differentiation hypothesis
The candidate must be narrower than HNSTP as previously phrased.

### HNCL — Human Need Continuity Ledger
Treat the need not simply as inferred context, a goal, a task, a user model or a digital twin, but as a governed canonical continuity object with explicit lifecycle and evidence.

Proposed state transition:

NEED_DECLARED/DETECTED
→ NEED_CLARIFIED
→ RIGHTS_AND_CONSENT_BOUND
→ JURISDICTION_AND_SAFETY_BOUND
→ SATISFACTION_CRITERIA_BOUND
→ CAPABILITY_SELECTED/COMPOSED/CREATED
→ EXECUTION_AUTHORIZED
→ EXECUTION_OBSERVED
→ OUTCOME_EVIDENCED
→ NEED_SATISFIED | PARTIAL | BLOCKED | CHANGED
→ CONTINUITY_HANDOFF

Each transition should retain immutable provenance and distinguish:
- what the human actually requested;
- what the system inferred;
- what was legally/policy permitted;
- what capability was used and why;
- what action actually occurred;
- what evidence proves the outcome;
- what remains unresolved;
- what may be carried across device/provider/app/kernel/language/jurisdiction boundaries.

### Candidate combined mechanism
HNCL + deterministic truth boundaries + capability composition/creation + zero-loss provenance + multi-kernel execution + relationship/context graph + cross-device/provider continuity.

The strongest differentiator is no longer 'AI understands human needs'. It is the possibility of a provider-neutral, evidence-bearing, rights-bounded continuity protocol in which a human need remains canonical while the software/agent/provider/device used to satisfy it can change.
