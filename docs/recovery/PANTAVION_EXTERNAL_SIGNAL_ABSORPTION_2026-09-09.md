## 2026-09-09 research decision — Gutenberg / Archive / AlternativeTo

### Project Gutenberg
Decision: **ACCEPT AS A LEGAL KNOWLEDGE-SOURCE PATTERN, NOT AS A NOVELTY CLAIM AND NOT AS A DIRECT SCRAPING TARGET.**

Verified constraints/patterns:
- The main Project Gutenberg website is intended for human users; automated bulk access should use its documented mirrors/robot harvest/catalog mechanisms rather than scraping ordinary pages.
- Machine-readable catalog data is explicitly offered and catalog metadata is public-domain data.
- Most Gutenberg ebooks are public domain in the United States, but copyright status can differ outside the US; Pantavion therefore must bind every ingestion decision to jurisdiction and item-level rights evidence rather than assuming global public-domain status.
- The Project Gutenberg trademark/license is separate from copyright status of individual works.

Pantavion mechanism to extract: **Rights-Bound Knowledge Ingestion**. A source adapter must emit source identity, acquisition route, work identity, jurisdiction, rights basis, license/trademark constraints, content fingerprint, acquisition timestamp and permitted operations before content becomes eligible for indexing/RAG/training/redistribution. Unknown rights => quarantine, not silent ingestion.

### Internet Archive
Decision: **ACCEPT AS A DISCOVERY/METADATA/PROVENANCE SOURCE PATTERN; RIGHTS MUST REMAIN ITEM-SPECIFIC.**

Verified useful pattern: Internet Archive advanced search can operate on item metadata and return machine-readable JSON/XML/CSV/RSS; full-text search exists for processed textual items. Pantavion should use documented interfaces and preserve source/item provenance. Presence in an archive is never treated as proof that Pantavion may copy, train on, redistribute or commercially exploit an item.

Pantavion mechanism to extract: **Archive Evidence Adapter** — query metadata -> resolve item -> capture provenance/rights metadata -> policy decision -> approved fetch/index operation. No anti-bot bypass and no rights inference from availability alone.

### AlternativeTo
Decision: **ACCEPT AS A TECHNOLOGY-DISCOVERY/RADAR SIGNAL, NOT AS AN AUTHORITATIVE CAPABILITY DATABASE.**

Verified pattern: AlternativeTo is a crowdsourced software-discovery service whose alternatives/recommendations are largely contributed by users. This is useful for competitor discovery and replacement-candidate generation, but crowdsourced rankings/opinions are not technical verification.

Pantavion mechanism to extract: **Capability Alternative Radar** — given a capability/provider, discover candidate alternatives -> verify each against primary source -> classify license/deployment/data/privacy/security/lock-in/cost -> sandbox/benchmark -> accept/reject. No candidate enters the provider-neutral capability mesh from popularity alone.

## Innovation-oriented synthesis candidates

These are **unverified candidates**, not novelty claims:

- **Intent-to-Capability Compiler**: natural human request -> typed intent -> specialist route -> bounded tools -> structured result -> evidence -> optional generated capability when an approved capability is missing.
- **Adversarial Truth Council**: multiple specialist evaluators independently challenge assumptions, evidence, logic, safety, legal constraints and downstream effects before a high-impact recommendation is accepted.
- **Executive Multi-Brain Governance Fabric**: responsibility-based specialist brains with measurable mandates, explicit authority envelopes, cross-checking and escalation instead of a flat swarm of agents.
- **Provider-Neutral Capability Mesh**: models, RAG, memory, tool adapters, automation and data stores are selected by capability contract rather than vendor identity, with fallback and migration paths.
- **Evidence-Bound Self-Evolution Loop**: external signals and internal failures can generate improvement candidates, but promotion requires provenance, simulation, benchmark, security review, policy review and rollback readiness.
- **Human-Native Intent Surface**: users need not memorize commands; the system recognizes intent from natural language while optional aliases provide fast expert control.
- **AI Visibility Knowledge Layer**: public product facts can be emitted as consistent, attributable, machine-readable knowledge for conventional and AI search without contaminating private/user memory.
- **Lawful Knowledge & Capability Discovery Fabric**: knowledge-source and software-capability discovery share provenance, rights/jurisdiction, primary-source verification, benchmark and promotion gates without conflating availability with permission or popularity with technical truth.
